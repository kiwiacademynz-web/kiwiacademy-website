IQN exam preparation for Ernakulam nurses

Online IQN coaching can help you review theoretical nursing knowledge and medication safety before the exam. Contact DREAMVISION to confirm the current class schedule, course outline and mock-test format.

Course preparation is separate from the NCNZ assessment. The exam is booked through an accredited Pearson VUE test centre; check current exam instructions and locations with NCNZ and Pearson VUE.

For the wider process, read the NCNZ competence pathway guide.
