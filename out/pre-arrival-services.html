Pre-arrival services

Arrive feeling more prepared with practical planning and coordination for your move to Australia or New Zealand.

  • Travel preparation
  • Accommodation planning
  • Arrival and settlement

Practical support, one step at a time

Make the move feel manageable

We can help you prepare for the practical details around departure and arrival. Services depend on your destination, timing and individual needs.

01

Visa preparation

Organise documents and understand application steps. Visa advice and decisions must come from the relevant government authority or a licensed immigration adviser.

02

Accommodation planning

Explore student housing, shared accommodation or temporary stays to help you plan your first days.

03

Pre-departure guidance

Prepare a destination-aware checklist for travel essentials, packing, money and local customs.

04

Flight planning

Compare travel options and organise your flight plans around your confirmed dates.

05

Airport pickup coordination

Discuss arrival transport arrangements so you know how you will get to your accommodation.

06

Orientation and settling in

Get an introduction to practical first steps such as transport, banking, healthcare and local services.

Share your destination, expected arrival date and the kind of support you need. We’ll help you work through the practical details.
