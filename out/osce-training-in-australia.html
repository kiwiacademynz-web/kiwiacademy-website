Preparation for your Australian pathway

Internationally qualified nurses can follow different Australian registration pathways. The assessment that applies, including whether an OSCE is required, depends on your individual circumstances and the current directions from Ahpra and the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA).

DREAMVISION offers OSCE training in Australia to help nurses practise clinical scenarios, communication, station routines and mock assessments. Training is independent preparation; it is not an Ahpra or NMBA assessment and does not determine eligibility or registration.

Australian OSCE training FAQs

Check your eligibility and assessment instructions with the relevant regulator.

Does every internationally qualified nurse need an OSCE?

No. Assessment requirements vary by pathway and individual circumstances. Use the official AHPRA self-check and follow the current Ahpra and NMBA directions.

Is this the official Australian assessment?

No. DREAMVISION provides preparation training only. Assessment and registration decisions are made by Ahpra and the NMBA.

Where in Australia is the training held?

Training is available in Australia. Contact DREAMVISION to confirm the current city, venue, dates and format before making travel plans.

Does OSCE training guarantee registration or an exam result?

No. Training supports preparation but cannot guarantee an assessment result or registration outcome.
