Understand your options before you apply

Choosing a course and destination is a major decision. We can help you clarify your goals, explore suitable study options and organise the steps involved in preparing an application.

  • Discuss your study goals, background and preferred location.
  • Understand the documents and application steps to prepare.
  • Get help organising your student visa application materials.
  • Plan for family members and practical arrangements after arrival.

Admission decisions, visa eligibility and visa outcomes are determined by education providers and New Zealand Immigration, not DREAMVISION. Immigration advice is provided only by appropriately licensed advisers.
