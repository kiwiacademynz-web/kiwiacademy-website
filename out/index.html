Your nursing journey

A clear pathway from preparation to registration.

Explore the stages and open each step to see how we can help you prepare.

  1. 01 Step 1English preparationOET / IELTS

    Prepare for the English-language test accepted for your target registration route. Test options and score requirements vary by regulator.

    Explore OET / IELTS preparation
  2. 02 Step 2Nursing examination preparationIQN / relevant assessment

    Prepare for the theoretical exam or other assessment that applies to your qualifications and registration route. For New Zealand IQN candidates, this may include the NCNZ theoretical examination.

    Explore IQN preparation
  3. 03 Step 3Clinical competence preparationOSCE / relevant Australian assessment

    If your regulator requires a clinical assessment, practise relevant skills, scenarios and mock stations. The assessment and preparation required depend on your individual pathway.

    Explore OSCE preparation Australian registration guidance
  4. 04 Step 4Registration and career guidanceNew Zealand / Australia

    Understand the remaining steps for your chosen destination and plan what comes next. Registration decisions are made by the relevant regulator.
